Prince
2 yrs · 115 lbs · Cane Corso Mix · Dallas, TX

Prince came to us from a cruelty case in west Texas. He was pulled from a situation no dog should endure — and despite everything, he is one of the most affectionate, gentle dogs we have ever had come through this rescue.

Prince is ready for a family who will give him the calm, loving home he never had. He does well with adults and older children, and has shown good behavior around other dogs. At 115 pounds he's a big presence, but a gentle one.

Caden
2 yrs · Blue Great Dane · Cruelty Case, Houston

Caden came to us from a cruelty case in Houston. He's a big, beautiful blue Great Dane who has come a long way since his rescue — fully obedience trained, and an amazing dog all around. Caden does need slow, calm introductions to new people at the front door, but once he warms up he's a true gem.

Bay
Caucasian Shepherd · Fort Worth Shelter

Bay was pulled from the Fort Worth shelter with minutes to spare. He'd been on the list, and JD got the call. He came home that day and never really left.

Bay has been with JD for over a year now — one of those dogs that changes what you think a rescue dog can be. Calm, loyal, perceptive. He's proof of what these dogs become when someone gives them a real chance. Bay is now available and looking for his forever home.
